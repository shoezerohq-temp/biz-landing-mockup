Order #6066, Purple Body vs Pink Arrows

Core Zero Low Tops · configs 399259 / 399257 · design link
Expected
Printed

Problem: body and arrow panels used two different purples, #ab51ff for the body and #c790fe for the arrow accent. On the printed shoe the arrows read as pink rather than a matching purple, so the two panels that should read as one purple family split into two visibly different colours. Four pairs were reprinted once photos confirmed the mismatch.

Reprinted

Orders #6124 / #6133, Fluorescent Purple Printed Darker

Core Zero Low Tops · config 405766 (order #6133) · design link · order #6124 has no design file on record
Expected
Printed

Problem: the design called for a light, slightly fluorescent purple at #8e77fb. The printed sample came back visibly darker and flatter than the on-screen colour. Fluorescent tones like this sit outside the colour range that standard printing (RGB screen colour translated to a printer's ink set) can reproduce, so an exact match is not achievable with normal equipment, only a closer approximation. This is a colour-gamut limit, not a one-off printing mistake.

Resolved, printed all white

Order #5570, Off-White vs White Split

Core Zero Low Tops · config 364195 · design link
Expected
Printed
Photo of printed pair to follow

Problem: one side of the shoe carried #edefed, a warm grey-white, and the other side pure #ffffff white. These two near-white shades are easy to confuse in a colour picker and on a screen, but print as two clearly different whites side by side on the same pair. This was caught before production and confirmed the pair should print all white. No camera photo of the finished pair is on file, every attachment on this order's record is either a design render or an unrelated cut-pattern sheet.

Resolved, design changed

Order #5389, Gradient Flagged Unpredictable

Classic Zero · config 331656 · design link
Expected
Printed
Photo of printed pair to follow

The gradient itself is inside the customer's uploaded artwork, not a panel colour, so there is no single hex for it. The two panel colours above are the shoe's flat fills.

Problem: the design used a colour gradient, a smooth blend between shades rather than one flat hex fill. Gradients shift and band unpredictably on print because small variations in ink lay-down show up more on a blend than on a flat colour. This was flagged before production and the design was revised to a flat colour instead. Both attachments on this order's record are studio renders, not camera photos, so no printed-pair photo is included here.

Resolved, redesigned

Order not on file, Lines Under 1mm Dropped

Model and config not on record for this order
Expected
design file not available
Printed

Problem: this is a line-weight failure rather than a wrong-colour failure, included here because thin lines and colour contrast are closely related print issues. Some artwork lines measured under 1mm wide and would not render cleanly at the print resolution used. Fix: thicken fine lines and raise contrast against the background, for example switching thin line art to white on a dark panel instead of a colour close to the background.
